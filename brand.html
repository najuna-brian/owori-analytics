The Owori Analytics identity

Logo files, colours, typography and tone of voice. Use this page for thumbnails, slides, social posts and printed material.

Logo

A bell curve inside the O

The ring is the O of Owori. Inside it sits a normal distribution, the first idea every statistics student meets, and its peak reads as the A of Analytics. The amber point marks the mean: the clear answer in the middle of the data.

Primary logo. Download SVG

Mark for avatars and favicons. Download SVG

  • Keep clear space around the logo of at least a quarter of the mark’s height.
  • Do not stretch, recolour, rotate or add effects. Use the reverse version on navy or on photographs.
  • The wordmark is set in Plus Jakarta Sans: “Owori” in ExtraBold and “Analytics” in Medium. Convert the text to outlines before sending the SVG to a printer.

Typography

Plus Jakarta Sans for headings

Inter for body text. It stays readable at small sizes, in tables and on slides full of numbers.

Both are free Google Fonts. Use sentence case for headings. Write statistics exactly, for example p = 0.003.

Voice
  • Clear. Plain words first, the technical term second.
  • Practical. Every idea ends in a step the learner can do.
  • Encouraging. Statistics is a skill anyone can learn, one test at a time.
  • Rigorous. Assumptions, limits and honest interpretation always get a mention.
